# Gul Ahmed winter brief (practice project)

Research notes for the Email Studio Lab, using Gul Ahmed's winter collection in place of the sample Kettlebird Tea brand. This is a learning exercise, not work for Gul Ahmed. Emails stay on this computer and go only to test addresses (`@example.com`, `@resend.dev`).

Source: gulahmedshop.com, read on 7 November 2026. Prices and sales change often, so treat every number here as a snapshot.

## The brand

- The online store calls itself **GulAhmed Ideas** ("Ideas"). It sells women's clothing, men's clothing, kids' clothing, bedding, fragrances, shoes and bags.
- Sub-brands: **Salt** (western wear for women, men and kids) and **Ideas Home** (bedding and bath linen).

## The winter collection

- The homepage hero is **"Charm of Winter"** (marked "Live now"), with the button "View Collection".
- 2026 winter collections featured on the site: Winter Charm 2026, Misty Meadows 2026, Crafted in Winter 2026, Whispers of the Season 2026, Arctic Aura 2026, Hello Winter 2026, A Winter Story 2026, My Winter Diaries 2026, Naqsh 2026.
- Main winter categories: women's unstitched (about 225 items), women's ready-to-wear, men's unstitched winter, Salt winter apparel for the family, and shawls.
- **Winter fabrics:** Khaddar (the biggest group, 89 items), Karandi, Pashmina, Linen, Jacquard, Corduroy, Viscose Slub, Karandi Velvet Jacquard.

## How the brand sounds (real site copy)

- "Where everyone starts — unstitched fabric, ready-to-wear, men's eastern and the pieces our customers come back for."
- "No hidden charges, no surprises."
- "We strive to give you a seamless shopping experience every time you shop with us."
- Collection names lean on season and feeling: Charm, Whispers, Diaries, Naqsh, Arctic Aura.

The tone is warm and elegant, with a focus on family and the season. It's polished but not loud.

## Facts the emails must get right

- **Shipping in Pakistan:** flat PKR 249 on every order. There's no free-shipping threshold, so never promise free delivery.
- **Delivery:** 3–5 business days after dispatch, plus up to 2 days to process the order. International orders go by DHL in 8–10 business days, and the customer pays duties.
- **Exchanges:** within 30 days of delivery (you must report it within 3 days). Pickup is free. Items must be unused with tags.
- **No cash refunds.** Refunds happen only for card orders cancelled early, or for faulty items. International orders can't be exchanged or returned.
- **Payment:** cash on delivery, card, and "Pay later" in 4 interest-free instalments.

## Claims to never make (ideas for brand/voice.md)

- Prices or discounts we can't confirm on the live site on the day the email goes out.
- "Free shipping" or "free delivery".
- "Easy refunds" or "money back".
- Fake urgency like "last chance" or "only 2 left" unless it's true.
- Claiming these emails come from Gul Ahmed. They're practice drafts.
