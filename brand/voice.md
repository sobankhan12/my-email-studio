# Gul Ahmed brand voice: winter emails

Practice voice guide for the Charm of Winter 2026 campaign. It's written for people aged 17–40 in Lahore, Multan, Karachi and Islamabad who buy unstitched fabric to have stitched.

Language: English mixed naturally with Urdu and Roman Urdu. Keep it easy to read. A Roman Urdu phrase should add feeling, not make the reader work to understand it.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Warm:** we sound like a trusted family brand talking to someone we know.
- **Fresh:** we sound modern and current, with new looks for a young reader.
- **Graceful:** we sound premium and proud of our fabric and craft, never loud.

## 2. Words we use

- Fabric names: **Khaddar, Karandi, Pashmina**, and other real fabrics from the collection (Linen, Jacquard, Corduroy).
- Feelings: **cozy, graceful, sukoon**.
- Roman Urdu phrases: **sardiyon ka rang, naya andaaz**.
- Collection names as they appear on the site, such as **Charm of Winter**.

## 3. Words we avoid

- Pushy sales words: "BUY NOW!!!", "hurry", "last chance", "cheapest".
- ALL-CAPS shouting, and more than one or two emojis in an email.
- Slang that's too casual: "bro", "lit" and similar.
- Words that make us sound cheap: "budget", "discount bin".

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **"Free delivery" or "free shipping".** Shipping in Pakistan is a flat PKR 249.
- **"Full refund" or "money back".** There are no cash refunds, only exchanges within 30 days. Whenever an email mentions exchanges, give the full conditions: tell us within 3 days of it arriving, and keep it unused with its tags. Pickup is free.
- **Delivery promises we can't keep**, like "wear it this week" or "arrives tomorrow". Orders take up to 2 days to prepare, then 3 to 5 business days to arrive.
- **Prices or "% off" that haven't been checked** on the live site the day the email goes out.
- **Fake urgency**, like "only 2 left" or "sale ends tonight", when it isn't true.
- **Health or comfort promises**, like "keeps you warm in any weather" or "100% pure Pashmina", unless the product page says so.

## 5. How we sign off

Every email ends with:

Sardiyon ki mohabbat ke saath,
Team Gul Ahmed
