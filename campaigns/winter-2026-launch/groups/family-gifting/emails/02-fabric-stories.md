A winter gift that feels personal starts with the fabric. This week, we want to tell you a little about the three at the heart of Charm of Winter 2026.

**Khaddar** is the classic winter fabric, with a soft, slightly textured feel. It holds prints and embroidery beautifully, which is why it's our biggest winter range.

**Karandi** has a fine, woven texture and a graceful drape. It makes a graceful gift for a bhabhi or khala to wear at the next family dinner.

**Pashmina** is the one for special evenings. It's soft, elegant and made to be noticed quietly.

You'll also find Linen, Jacquard and Corduroy in the collection, for anyone who likes something a little different.

**A small tip from us:** tailors get busy as winter gatherings and weddings come closer. If you love a fabric, stitching it early means your outfit is ready when you are, with nothing to worry about. That's real sukoon.
