# Campaign plan: Charm of Winter 2026 launch

Practice campaign for the Email Studio Lab. Emails go to made-up `@example.com` leads and test addresses only. Voice and forbidden claims come from `brand/voice.md`.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Sardiyon Ka Rang: Charm of Winter 2026
- **Summary:** a four-week email series that introduces our mailing list to the Charm of Winter 2026 collection and helps each reader find the winter look that fits them.
- **Main goal:** **100 mailing-list members place a winter order between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.** Stretch target: 150.
- **Other goals:**
  - Learn which of the three groups responds best, to shape the next campaign.
  - Keep unsubscribes low by sending one useful email a week, not a flood.

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 people on our mailing list, aged 17–40, living in Lahore, Multan, Karachi and Islamabad.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | Where they are in buying |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `unstitched-lovers` | Buy fabric and have it stitched to their own design | Good winter fabric (Khaddar, Karandi, Pashmina) and prints that feel new | Ready to buy, but need time for tailoring |
| `ready-to-wear` | Want a finished outfit without visiting a tailor | Easy, stylish pieces they can wear this week | Ready to buy, just need the right piece |
| `family-gifting` | Shop for parents, siblings and in-laws in winter | Gifts that feel thoughtful and graceful | Still deciding what to give |

**What they care about:** staying cozy without looking plain, a naya andaaz for winter gatherings and weddings, and fabric they can trust.

**What holds them back:** tailoring takes time, they aren't sure what suits the gift receiver, and they worry about delivery time and exchanges.

**Where they spend time:** Instagram, WhatsApp, and their email inbox. This plan uses email only.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** this winter, find your sardiyon ka rang in fabric made to feel cozy and look graceful.

| Supporting message | For | Proof point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Our winter fabrics are made for the season: Khaddar, Karandi and Pashmina. | unstitched-lovers | The fabric names and details on the collection pages |
| Stitch early so your outfit is ready for winter gatherings. | unstitched-lovers | Tailors get busy in December |
| Ready-to-wear gives you the naya andaaz without the wait. | ready-to-wear | The ready-to-wear winter range on the site |
| A winter outfit is a gift that feels personal. | family-gifting | 30-day exchanges help if the size or colour isn't right |
| Clear, honest shopping: flat delivery fee, cash on delivery, exchanges within 30 days. | everyone | Shipping and exchange policy pages |

**Tone:** warm, fresh and graceful, in English mixed with Urdu and Roman Urdu, as set in `brand/voice.md`.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why it fits | Format | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Email (owned) | The audience already chose to hear from us, and it's the only channel this plan uses | 4 weekly emails, each with one version per group | Low to medium |

Paid ads, social media and influencers are out of scope for this plan.

## 5. Content calendar

All four emails go out on Mondays (dates checked with a calendar command). Each email goes to all three groups, and each group gets its own version (made in Step 8 with the `personalize` skill).

| Week | Send date | Email | Audience | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Monday 7 December 2026 | 01: Charm of Winter is here | All three groups | Announce the collection and show the core message | Not started |
| 2 | Monday 14 December 2026 | 02: Fabric stories | All three groups (lead angle: unstitched-lovers) | Bring Khaddar, Karandi and Pashmina to life, and suggest stitching early | Not started |
| 3 | Monday 21 December 2026 | 03: Winter gatherings and gifting | All three groups (lead angle: family-gifting) | Ideas for gifts and outfits for family gatherings over the winter holidays | Not started |
| 4 | Monday 28 December 2026 | 04: Your winter look, sorted | All three groups (lead angle: ready-to-wear) | Round up the favourite looks, and remind readers about exchanges and cash on delivery | Not started |

**Dependencies:**
- Collection page links must be confirmed before Monday 7 December (practice link: `https://gulahmed.example/winter`).
- Any price or offer mentioned must be checked on the live site the day the email goes out, as `brand/voice.md` requires.
- Each email is written two to three working days before its send date, then reviewed in a fresh session.

## 7. Success metrics

| Metric | Target | How it's tracked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Winter orders from the mailing list** (main) | **100 by Sunday 3 January 2027** (stretch: 150) | Orders that came through the campaign link |
| Open rate | 35% or more | Email platform reports |
| Click rate | 4% or more | Clicks on the one link in each email |
| Orders per group | Compare the three groups | Separate link tag per group |
| Unsubscribe rate | Under 0.5% per email | Email platform reports |

**Reporting:** a short check every Tuesday after each send, and one wrap-up on Monday 4 January 2027.

## 8. Spend and effort

- **Spend:** small. No paid media. Costs are the time to write, review and send.
- **Production:** about 2–3 working days per email, including the fresh-session review.
- **Spare time:** keep about 15% of the schedule free to fix problems found in review.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | What could happen | What we do about it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The goal is off | 100 orders from 2,000 people is a 5% order rate across four emails. A warm list may do better or worse | Check week 1 results. If we're on track, aim for the 150 stretch; if not, steer emails 2–4 toward the group responding best |
| Wrong promises | An email states a price, offer or delivery promise the site doesn't support | Every email is checked against `brand/voice.md` section 4 before it goes out |
| Tailoring time | Unstitched buyers order too late to stitch before gatherings | Email 02 suggests stitching early, and email 04 leans on ready-to-wear |
| Too many emails | Readers tune out or unsubscribe | Keep to one email a week, each with a clear reason to open |
